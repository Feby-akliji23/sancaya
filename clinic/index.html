Join Us Now

At Sancaya Indonesia, we proudly operate a child development clinic as part of our commitment to fostering children's growth and development in alignment with their developmental milestones. By focusing on accurate diagnosis, early prevention, and tailored interventions, we aim to support children’s holistic development following the learning pyramid framework. In collaboration with esteemed specialists, professionals, and researchers from leading global universities, we strive to provide the highest quality of care and support for every child. Our clinic is dedicated to serving both enrolled students and the wider community, and we sincerely hope to make a positive impact on the lives of many children.

Our Services

1.Speech Language Therapy
Unlock communication potential with our expert speech-language therapy services. Our licensed therapists provide personalized, evidence-based care for all ages, addressing:
  • Speech sound disorders
  • Language development
  • Stuttering and fluency
  • Voice and swallowing issues
  • Cognitive-communication skills
  • Augmentative communication (AAC)
2.Occupational Therapy
Thrive in daily life with our personalized occupational therapy.
  • Fine motor and coordination
  • Sensory processing
  • Daily living activities
  • Cognitive and physical function
  • Adaptive equipment use
3.Behavior Therapy
Transform lives with our expert Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) therapy. Our certified therapists deliver personalized, evidence-based interventions to help individuals of all ages develop essential skills and achieve meaningful behavior change.
  • Communication and social skills
  • Behavior management
  • Learning and academic readiness
  • Independence and daily living
4.Learning and Academic Support
Enhance academic success with our tailored learning support. Our skilled educators assist students of all ages with:
  • Reading, writing, and math skills
  • Study and organizational strategies
  • Homework and test preparation
  • Learning challenges (such as dyslexia, ADHD, etc.)
5.Pediatric Developmental Assessments
Ensure your child’s growth is on track with our comprehensive pediatric developmental assessments. Our experienced specialists provide thorough evaluations to support your child’s physical, cognitive, and social-emotional development.
  • Developmental Milestones
  • Early Intervention
  • Behavioral Assessments
  • Learning Abilities
  • Sensory Processing
6.Mental Health Counseling
Find balance and well-being with our compassionate mental health counseling services. Our licensed therapists provide personalized, evidence-based support to help children navigate life’s challenges.
  • Anxiety & Stress
  • Depression
  • Trauma & Grief
  • Relationship Issues
  • Self-Esteem
  • Life Transitions
7.Art and Music Therapy
Discover healing and self-expression through our art and music therapy services. We use creative approaches to support emotional, cognitive, and social well-being for all ages.
  • Art Therapy: Using visual arts to process emotions and build confidence.
  • Music Therapy: Engaging with music to enhance communication and mood.
  • Emotional Expression: Supporting stress, anxiety, and trauma recovery.
  • Cognitive Development: Improving focus, memory, and problem-solving.
  • Social Skills: Fostering connection through creative collaboration.
8.Neurosensory
Enhance sensory processing and neurological well-being with our specialized neurosensory therapy. Our expert therapists provide personalized, evidence-based interventions to support children. We offer targeted therapy for:
  • Sensory Processing: Addressing sensitivities to sound, touch, or movement.
  • Motor Coordination: Improving balance, motor planning, and fine motor skills.
  • Neurodevelopmental Support: Assisting with autism, ADHD, or developmental delays.
  • Cognitive Enhancement: Boosting attention, memory, and executive function.
  • Emotional Regulation: Managing sensory-related stress or anxiety.
9.Physiotherapy
Help your child move, grow, and thrive with our specialized pediatric physiotherapy services. Our skilled therapists provide personalized, evidence-based care to support children’s physical development and mobility. We offer tailored physiotherapy for:
  • Motor Development: Supporting gross motor skills, balance, and coordination.
  • Developmental Delays: Addressing delays in crawling, walking, or other milestones
  • Injury Recovery: Rehabilitating sprains, fractures, or sports injuries.
  • Neurological Conditions: Assisting with cerebral palsy, spina bifida, or other conditions.
  • Posture & Strength: Improving core strength and alignment.
