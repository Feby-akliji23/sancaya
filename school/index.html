Foundation

Our foundation curriculum is aimed at promoting holistic development, equity, and a lifelong passion for learning. By embracing the principles of inclusivity and student-centered education, it establishes a flexible framework for early childhood education and care (ECEC) as well as basic education. This approach prioritizes well-being, creativity, and critical thinking, moving beyond traditional rote memorization to foster a more engaging and effective learning experience for all students.

Primary

Our curriculum provides a flexible, student-centered framework that fosters holistic development, critical thinking, and equity. Aligned with the Indonesia and Australia Curriculum, it integrates core subjects such as Indonesian, English, mathematics, sciences, arts, and physical education with key skills like multiliteracy, digital skills, and sustainability. Through interdisciplinary, phenomenon-based learning, students tackle real-world problems. Formative assessments emphasize constructive feedback to promote growth. Supported by trained teachers who tailor lessons to local contexts, we nurture an inclusive environment for diverse learners, fostering curious and resilient thinkers.

Secondary

Our inclusive secondary education ensures that students of all abilities, backgrounds, and learning needs receive equal opportunities to learn, participate, and succeed in a mainstream school setting. .It emphasizes diversity, accessibility, and individualized support while preparing students for further education, employment, and independent living. We emphasize a holistic, student-centered approach that integrates real-world applications and collaborative learning. It focuses on developing students' social skills, learning-to-learn capabilities, and critical thinking alongside subject-matter knowledge. Cross-curricular themes, phenomenon-based learning, and diverse learning methods are key components.

Special Education

Toward Independence Program

Our Toward Independence Program is dedicated to fostering essential independence skills, including life skills as well as sensory and motor development, through in-school therapy. In partnership with our school clinic, this initiative is designed for children with special needs who benefit from additional guidance and intensive focus on sensory and motor skills before transitioning to our regular school program. The program places emphasis on self-development, comprehensive therapy, and emotional growth, ensuring that each child receives the support they need to thrive.
