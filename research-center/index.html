Our Research Center

Be part of our mission for a sustainable, inclusive, and equitable future! Join our research in organic farming, early intervention, and inclusive education.

Join Us Now

At Sancaya Indonesia, we are committed to improving the lives of children through collaborative research and partnerships with esteemed universities. Our approach emphasizes accurate diagnoses, personalized development plans, and effective interventions to ensure that every child receives the essential support they deserve. We also place significant importance on a dietary and nutritional foundation as a cornerstone of our therapy. In this regard, we advocate for organic farming practices through permaculture and biodynamics, striving to guarantee that all children have access to nutritious, pesticide-free food. We believe that the impact of our interventions can be greatly enhanced when children receive the proper nutrition they require.

Our Services

1.Children Development Center

This program is dedicated to advancing the understanding of child development through innovative research, education, and community engagement. Our mission is to foster healthy growth and development in children by bridging science, policy, and practice.

We offer a variety of programs to translate research into action:

  • Parent Education Workshops
  • Teacher Training
  • Community Outreach
  • Research Fellowships
2.Organic Farming

Welcome to our Organic Farming Research Program, focused on advancing sustainable agriculture. Our mission is to enhance organic practices that nourish the soil, protect ecosystems, and provide healthy food to communities worldwide.

Our team collaborates to:

  • Develop eco-friendly techniques that improve soil health.
  • Optimize crop yields using chemical-free methods.
  • Examine the impacts of organic systems.
  • Share knowledge to promote sustainable practices.

Join us, whether as a farmer, student, or sustainability advocate. Explore our research, attend workshops, or partner with us to shape the future of organic farming.

3.Early Intervention for Special Needs

Welcome to our Early Intervention for Special Needs Research Program, dedicated to supporting children with special needs through timely, evidence-based strategies. Our mission is to advance early intervention that empowers children, families, and communities to promote developmental progress and inclusion.

Our interdisciplinary team collaborates to:

  • Develop innovative intervention techniques for diverse developmental needs.
  • Identify critical intervention windows for optimal growth.
  • Evaluate programs addressing autism, developmental delays, and other special needs.
  • Provide resources and training for families, caregivers, and professionals.

Join us in creating a brighter future for children with special needs. Explore our research, participate in workshops, or collaborate with us to enable meaningful change.

4.Inclusive Education

Welcome to our Inclusive Education Research Program, focused on creating equitable learning environments for every student. Our mission is to advance inclusive education practices that empower all students to succeed.

We develop evidence-based strategies to:

  • Design accessible curricula and inclusive teaching practices.
  • Identify and evaluate the impact of inclusive education policies.
  • Provide training and resources for educators, administrators, and families.

Whether you’re an educator, parent, student, or advocate, you can help shape the future of inclusive education. Explore our research, attend workshops, or collaborate with us to build schools where every learner belongs.
