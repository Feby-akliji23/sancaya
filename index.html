Sancaya Indonesia believes that quality education is a right for everyone, regardless of their background, economic status, or any other factors that may limit access to education. Our goal is to ensure that every child has the opportunity to reach their full potential—academically, emotionally, and socially. This is where Sancaya Indonesia plays a vital role by providing essential support for education within the community.

We strive to create a 100% inclusive environment where everyone is supported and celebrated.

We invite you to visit us and become a part of our community. At Sancaya Indonesia, we believe in the power of kindness.

Our School

Foundation - Primary - Secondary - SPED

Sancaya Indonesia is an inclusive school in Bali, Indonesia, welcoming students from all backgrounds—including mainstream learners and those with additional learning needs. Rooted in harmony among nature, humanity, and compassion, we blend the Indonesian and Australian curricula through a holistic, personalized approach so every child learns with confidence. With small classes, individualized education plans (IEPs) or tailored learning goals as appropriate, and close teacher–therapist–parent collaboration, we support sensory development, physical health, and social-emotional skills in a calm, sensory-friendly environment. Our mission is to nurture individuals who are fulfilled, motivated, and committed to creating a better world.

Our Research Center

At Sancaya Indonesia we advance child development through collaborative research and partnerships with leading universities. Our multidisciplinary team delivers accurate assessments, personalized development plans, and evidence-based interventions so every child gets the support they deserve. We also anchor therapy in nutrition, championing permaculture and biodynamic organic farming to ensure access to pesticide-free, nutrient-dense foods. This integrated model—research, practice, and nutrition—helps children learn confidently and grow in overall health and well-being.

Our Services to The Community

Community service sits at the heart of our values fostering compassion, nurturing leadership, and instilling responsibility. Through a structured service-learning program, we collaborate with local partners, design real volunteer projects, and create measurable impact across the school and wider community. This hands-on approach builds lifelong skills—empathy, teamwork, communication, and problem-solving while strengthening civic engagement and empowering young people to lead meaningful change.
