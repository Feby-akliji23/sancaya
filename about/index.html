About Us

Kindness and Compassion is a cornerstone of Sancaya Indonesia. It involves not just feeling empathy for others, but actively showing kindness, understanding, and support to all members of our community.

By donating or enrolling in our regular school program, you play a crucial role in fostering meaningful change while gaining access to quality education. Your participation supports a variety of our foundation’s initiatives, including:

  • Education for Local Children: Providing high-quality educational opportunities for children who need them the most.
  • Support for Special Needs: Offering essential therapies and resources for children with special needs to assist in their development.
  • Inclusive Education Research: Advancing research and programs that advocate for inclusive education for every child.
  • Sustainable Organic Farming: Promoting environmentally sustainable practices and research in agriculture.
  • Community Programs: Strengthening local community initiatives to create a positive social impact.

Vision

To create a harmonious world where humanity flourishes in balance with nature, and every individual enjoys equitable access to inclusive education and a sustainable environment. We envision a future where empowered communities protect the planet, embrace diversity, and cultivate lifelong learning, creating a just and thriving world for generations to come.

Mission

To empower humanity through inclusive education and environmental stewardship. We aim to break down learning barriers, promote sustainable practices, and foster a connection with the planet. By integrating education and environmental action, we inspire individuals and communities to be catalysts for positive change, ensuring a sustainable future for all.
