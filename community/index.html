Our Community

From fields to classrooms, your expertise matters. Join our volunteer team—across Sunday Services, Community Kitchen, and Teacher Training—to turn ideas into real impact.

Join Our Volunteer

Our Community Programs

1.Sunday Services

Our Sunday Service fosters a meaningful connection with nature. We engage children in activities such as river cleanups, recycling initiatives, organic farming, and education on sustainable living practices. The event culminates with a communal meal featuring food sourced from our organic farm. Our aim is to instill in our children a deep appreciation for the environment and the significance of preserving our natural world.

2.Community Kitchen

Our Community Kitchen program is dedicated to providing nutritious meals to children in our neighborhood and those facing challenges. We prepare meals daily and distribute them every Sunday to those in need. Our aim is to exemplify and promote the enduring values of compassion and generosity within our community. By doing so, we hope to inspire others to uphold these important principles. Additionally, we strive to offer support to individuals experiencing financial difficulties, helping to alleviate their burdens.

3.Scholarship Program

We are pleased to offer a scholarship program designed to provide support to deserving children from low-income families, enabling them to access high-quality educational opportunities through our various programs. This initiative encompasses scholarships for participation in our formal education program, afterschool activities, and therapeutic services. We firmly believe in the principles of compassion and the fundamental right to education for every child.

4.Teacher Training

As part of our initiative, we are dedicated to offering comprehensive training for schools and educators who seek to enhance their understanding of inclusive education. We believe that equipping teachers with the necessary knowledge and skills will enable a greater number of children to access the tailored support and learning strategies they require. Our vision is to foster an educational environment where more teachers and schools are well-prepared and receptive to the principles of inclusive education in the future.

Why Volunteer With Us?

Contribute to innovative research in sustainable agriculture and education. Collaborate with leading researchers and educators. Gain hands-on experience in field studies and community outreach. Make a difference in local and global communities.

Volunteer Opportunities

We offer flexible roles, including:

  • Field Support: Assist with organic farming trials and community gardens.
  • Program Assistance: Support programs for early intervention and inclusive education.
  • Outreach: Help raise awareness through events and social media.
  • Administrative Support: Organize events and manage resources.

Who Can Volunteer?

Everyone with a passion for sustainability and education is welcome, regardless of experience. Your commitment matters!

Get Started:

  1. Sign Up: Complete our online form to share your interests.
  2. Learn More: Attend an info session to explore how you can contribute.
  3. Connect: Reach out with questions or ideas.
